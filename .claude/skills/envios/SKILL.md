# Envíos (boxes) and routes

## Envio — one physical box, one supplier

- Route (`modo`) and supplier (`supplierId`, `null` = 99rpm at the base ₹ price) are chosen at creation and **never change**. The box is the purchase: lines inherit its supplier, `origen` and `inbound` in `asignarAEnvio`.
- **`aereo`** — the commercial lane: carries `PedidoItem` (client orders and own stock). Born `borrador`; `confirmarCajaAerea` makes it a purchase. In `borrador` nothing counts as inventory and nothing can be bought, paid or moved (`motivoCajaEnBorrador`). `volverCajaABorrador` only while nothing was bought, costed or paid. (Old boxes stayed `confirmado`; only `createEnvio` sets `borrador`.)
- **`maritimo_cbm`** — the stocking lane: carries `EnvioLinea` (own merchandise: product + quantity, no price, no client). Filled while `borrador` (`sincronizarLineas` refuses discontinued parts), closed, received (adds to `Product.stock`). Reopening is refused once a payment is recorded against it — same rule as the air lane.
- Unconfirmed client quotes can sit in a box (to see who funds it) but are never bought (`registrarCompra` and the per-box purchase list filter `status = 'pedido'`).

**A bought line does not leave its box.** "Quitar" (`removePedido`) only releases lines that are `pendiente` with no `costRealUsd` and reports what stayed; `deleteEnvio` refuses a box with bought or moving lines or with ledger entries; a line bought outside a box cannot enter a box that has a supplier. Releasing one left the box "overpaid", the piece out of in-transit inventory, and free to be re-costed under another supplier.

**Moving an order to another box (`moverPedidoACaja`, rules in `lib/mover-caja.ts`)** is the one exception: to split a box at the depot into two shipments, each with its own fixed costs and invoice. The picker always offers "Caja nueva" when the origin can be split: `destinoId = 'nueva'` creates the sibling (confirmed, same supplier, mode, `reenvio`, `tarifaKgInr`, named "… · parte N") inside the same transaction, so a refusal leaves no empty box. When the origin itself can't be split, "Mover" is a button that prints the reason (no hover on the phone). The order's lines move as they are (stage, `costRealUsd`, supplier, route). Both boxes must be `aereo` or both `express`, same supplier and `reenvio`, origin confirmed (a draft just uses Quitar), destination confirmed, not a `cotizado` supplier, nothing (non-landed) at `camino_usa` or later in either box, and **no air freight paid** on either (billed is fine: it is corrected per box). Guarded: both `Envio` rows locked in id order, `updateMany` by box and stage, count must match. The two boxes stay **one purchase**: the destination gets `compraDeId` = the root box (flat: always the root), and `cuentasPorPagar` and the box page add owed and paid across the family (payments stay where they were recorded). A box from another split purchase is refused as destination. A joining box with no commissions gets explicit `0` (the wire's commissions live on the root). If the origin had `llegada` boxes, it is switched to `reempaque` so its costing is `provisoria` until the outgoing boxes are loaded.

## Amounts nothing can derive (all nullable — empty is "not loaded", never zero)

- `tramoUsd` — the flat total a `cotizado` supplier billed to reach the USA. Missing ⇒ the box is flagged under-costed.
- `reenvio` (`shoppre` | `por_kilo`, chosen at creation, forced to `shoppre` for a `cotizado` supplier) + `tarifaKgInr` — which depot carries the box to the USA; the per-kilo one quotes ₹/kg per box (`guardarTarifaKg`). Same route and stages as Shoppre (labels say "depósito"). `cambiarReenvio` switches it only while `motivoNoCambiaReenvio` allows (no non-landed line at `camino_usa` or later, no air freight billed or paid; guarded `updateMany`), keeping the rate. Bought lines may switch: the owner picks the depot address when buying. The box page compares both lanes estimate-to-estimate and shows `tarifaEquilibrioKgInr`, the highest ₹/kg worth accepting.
- `comisionSalienteUsd` (my bank, known the same day) and `comisionEntranteUsd` (correspondent/supplier bank on arrival — shows up as the supplier saying he got less). Two columns because they arrive at different times; both are my cost and both enter the landed. An explicit `0` is a finding. `giro.cargada` = both recorded. **No commission rule on the supplier**: a stored percentage would be an invented number flowing into prices. The sea lane uses the same columns and form.
- `EnvioCaja` + `Envio.reempaque` (`lib/cajas-envio.ts`) — the physical boxes as the carrier/depot weighed them: one delivery often arrives in several parcels. `etapa` `llegada` = what reached the depot; `salida` = the boxes leaving after a repack (only with `reempaque`). Costing uses `cajasQueSeFacturan`: `salida` when repacked, else `llegada`; repacked with no `salida` yet ⇒ `llegada`, flagged `provisoria`. They **replace** the summed pieces (see the `costeo` skill). `guardarCajas` replaces the whole list (like trackings) and touches nothing else; with `reempaque` off the `salida` rows are deleted. Each row may be partial (a reference with no weight is still traceability); dims go all three or none; unreadable numbers are rejected, `0` = no data. Max 20 per stage. UI (`components/CajasEnvio.tsx`): one summary line in the box page, editing in a pop-up (portalled; Cancel discards) with an explicit No/Sí repack choice.
- `EnvioTracking`: tracking numbers per leg (`local` supplier → depot in India, `usa`, `venezuela`), each with an optional carrier, up to 10 per leg (`lib/tracking.ts`). `guardarTrackings` replaces the box's whole list but **keeps** a row with the same leg + number + courier (its Ship24 state and `trackerId`). Searchable in `/buscar`. **Not shown on the order lines** (owner's call: a big order has several local and international trackings and a line can't say which one carries its piece); the order links to the box.
- **Ship24** (`lib/ship24.ts` pure + API, `lib/sync-trackings.ts` DB; replaced TrackingMore on 2026-11-01): the carrier is **picked**, not typed: `ElegirCourier` searches `TrackingCourier` (Ship24's `GET /couriers` minus deprecated codes, refreshed weekly; `buscarCouriers`) and sends the `courierCode`. Text left unpicked is **rejected** by `courierDeFila` (a typo must not silently lose tracking) unless it is an exact unique name/code or the owner chose "anotar sin seguimiento" (Zoom, Tealca, a Shoppre reference). Without a list (no API key) it is free text. `pnpm tracking:sync` (hourly, `bajaj-tracking.timer`) creates each row's tracker once with `POST /trackers/track` (one shipment of the plan's quota; the body comes from `cuerpoTracker` and never changes, because Ship24 is idempotent only for identical fields and any change makes a new tracker), stores `trackerId`, then polls `GET /trackers/{id}/results`. It copies `statusMilestone` to `estado` (`detenido` when `isTracked` turns false without delivery), `statusCode` to `subestado`, and the events; `delivered`/`detenido` stop being polled. A courier code missing from the list, an invalid number (5–50 chars, `A-Z0-9-_/.`, spaces stripped) or a per-row API error (`validation_error`, `parcel_not_found`…) is stored and not retried until the box's "Actualizar ahora"; key, plan, quota, 429 and 5xx stop the run. Writes are guarded by id+number+courier. The full checkpoint list opens in a pop-up per tracking in the box (`PasosTracking`, portalled to `body`). It is a **copy of the courier's word: it never moves stages, stock or money**. Polling, not webhook: the app sits behind oauth2-proxy and a box takes weeks. Off without `SHIP24_API_KEY`. Rows without a courier are not sent even though Ship24 can auto-detect: each tracker costs quota and many are not trackable (Zoom, Shoppre references).

**Billed freight (`lib/flete-real.ts`)**: `shippingCostRealAereo/Maritimo` is what each carrier billed and **is the cost**. "Paid" is never stored: it is derived from `Movimiento` egresos `flete_aereo | flete_maritimo` with `envioId`. `pagarFlete` locks the `Envio` row and refuses paying past the billed amount; paying with nothing billed stores the payment as billed. A `cotizado` box has no air-freight row (that leg is `tramoUsd`, paid inside the wire).

**Who funds a box (`lib/financiamiento-envio.ts`)**: cost split into clients / own stock / unapproved quotes; a client's advance is prorated by the share of the order travelling in this box. Gross margin is over client lines only (an own-stock `salePrice` is an estimate).

## Routes (`lib/shipping-status.ts`)

`SHIPPING_STATUSES` is the ordered pipeline; routes are subsets in the same order, so `statusIndex` compares across routes.

| Route | Stages |
|---|---|
| `shoppre` | pendiente → camino_shoppre → en_shoppre → camino_usa → en_usa → camino_venezuela → en_venezuela → entregado |
| `cotizado` | pendiente → camino_usa → … (skips Shoppre) |
| `landed` | pendiente → en_venezuela → entregado (`isLanded`: never travels in the box) |
| `express` | pendiente → camino_shoppre → en_shoppre → camino_venezuela → en_venezuela → entregado (the box's `modo = 'express'`: no USA) |

**Express box (`Envio.modo = 'express'`)**: Shoppre ships straight from its India depot to Venezuela. It behaves like the air box (`llevaPedidos`, `MODOS_CON_PEDIDOS`: borrador → confirm, buy, pay, move) but `routeFor(inbound, isLanded, modo)` gives the `express` route, nothing in it is landed, the supplier must go through the depot (no `cotizado`), and there is **no maritime leg**: one freight row (Shoppre's invoice for the whole trip, stored in `shippingCostRealAereo`), no `usa` tracking. It carries **only express quotes**, and express quotes travel only in it (`motivoViaNoCoincide`, checked in `asignarAEnvio`; the box page and "add all confirmed" filter by `Pedido.via`). `confirmarCajaAerea` refuses an express box over `express_tope_usd` (`topeDeCaja` + `motivoExpressNoAprobable`). `/resultados` treats it as fully billed once Shoppre's invoice is in.

What separates the first two is whether goods pass through Shoppre's warehouse, not the country (`lib/inbound.ts`). `routeFor(inbound, isLanded)` picks the route, `nextStatus` skips inapplicable stages, `normalizeToRoute` repairs a line left on a stage its route lacks. `PedidoItem.inbound` keeps a bought line on the route it was bought with.

## Moving lines (`saveItemChanges` → `lib/mover-etapas.ts`)

One SQL statement per batch: the guarded `UPDATE … WHERE shippingStatus = desde` (a double click is a no-op), the `PedidoItemEtapa` history row, and — for own-stock lines crossing `entregado` — the `Product.stock` credit/debit by pieces (`lib/stock-piezas.ts`; a bundle credits its pieces, resolved by `bajajCode`, or by name only when the name belongs to one product — an unresolvable piece refuses the whole delivery, never a half credit). Taking a line out of `entregado` when that stock was sold is refused. `pnpm check:etapas` tests this SQL against the Docker DB.

**What the supplier didn't send**: open `sin_enviar` incidents (`sinEnviarDe`) are subtracted from the box count (`piezasAContar`, shown as "+N sin enviar"), a line not sent at all (`lineaNoViene`) is left out of "Marcar recibido" (received by hand when it shows up, after closing the incident as `llego`), and the items table tags the line. Costing and weight are not touched (the box was paid for those units). Applies to client and own-stock lines alike. "Paid to the supplier" (box page and `cuentasPorPagar`) is `pago_proveedor`/`comision_giro` egresos **minus** `reembolso_proveedor` ingresos of the box.

Before moving: cancelled orders don't move; an open `no_disponible` blocks buying, any open incident blocks delivery. **A client line stops at `en_venezuela` ("Recibido")**: the box refuses `entregado` for it (`seEntregaDesdeElPedido`) and the table doesn't offer it; it is delivered from the order with `confirmarEntrega` (see the `pedidos` skill). Taking one *out* of `entregado` is still allowed here, as a correction. Own stock is still delivered (= credited to stock) in the box.

`/envios` shows real times per stage (`lib/etapas.ts`) once there is history; regressions and gaps are not measured as legs.
